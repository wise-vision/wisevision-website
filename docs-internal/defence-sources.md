# Defence page: public sources and the exact text we rely on

Every number or policy statement on `/defence/` (`content/copy/defence.md`) cites one of these. Each URL was
opened with `web_extract` on 2026-09-29 and the quote below is copied from the extracted page. If a source
changes or disappears, the matching claim (`def-*` in `CLAIMS.md`) must be reworded or removed.

**H5:** Adam reads the defence page once after launch (plan §6 risk 3). **Export control:** EU dual-use
rules are flagged for counsel if a defence buyer engages; the page says only "Dual-use export rules apply".

## 1. Ministry of Defence of Ukraine: UGV missions (claim `def-ugv-missions`)

- URL: https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/robots-at-work-defence-forces-of-ukraine-more-than-triple-their-use-of-ug-vs
- Published: 7 September 2026. Licence: CC BY 4.0 (site footer).
- Quote: "The number of unmanned ground vehicle (UGV) missions has increased 3.3-fold since the beginning of 2026.
  In August alone, the UGVs carried out more than 25,000 logistics and evacuation missions on the front line."
  Monthly series: "January: 7,511 … August: 25,143." "These figures are based on data from the DELTA combat system."
- Our sentence: "In August 2026, Ukraine's ground robots ran more than 25,000 logistics and evacuation missions in a
  single month, 3.3 times the January figure."

## 2. Defence Procurement Agency DOT via zbroya.gov.ua: UGV contracts (claim `def-ugv-contracts`)

- URL: https://www.zbroya.gov.ua/en/news/ponad-22-000-nrk-zakontraktovano-z-pochatku-2026-roku-maizhe-udvichi-bilshe-nizh-za-ves-mynulyi
- Published: 20 July 2026.
- Quote: "the Defence Procurement Agency DOT has already contracted over 22,000 unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) for the
  Defence Forces for 2026. That is nearly double the total for all of last year." Also: "The overwhelming majority
  are logistics systems, followed by engineering systems, and then combat systems."
- Our sentence: "By July 2026, Ukraine's procurement agency had contracted over 22,000 unmanned ground vehicles for
  the year, nearly double the total for all of 2025."

## 3. Defense News: the 100% robotic logistics goal (claim `def-logistics-goal`)

- URL: https://www.defensenews.com/unmanned/2026/04/24/ukraine-to-field-25000-ground-robots-in-push-to-replace-soldiers-for-frontline-logistics
- Published: 24 April 2026, Katie Livingstone.
- Quote: "“Our goal — 100% of frontline logistics should be performed by robotic systems,” the minister said."
  (Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov.)
- Our sentence: Ukraine's defence minister set the target publicly: "100% of frontline logistics should be
  performed by robotic systems."

## 4. RBC-Ukraine: how electronic warfare breaks the link (claim `def-ew`)

- URL: https://newsukraine.rbc.ua/news/how-ukrainian-drones-use-ai-and-autopilot-1783239853.html
- Published: 5 July 2026, Vladyslava Kovalenko.
- Quote: "The first is jamming. EW systems generate powerful radio interference that severs communication between the
  drone and its ground control station. The second is GPS spoofing, which involves transmitting false satellite
  signals." Also, relevant to our principle: "AI cannot be entrusted with selecting targets on its own. It could
  make mistakes and strike civilians or civilian vehicles".
- Our sentence: "Electronic warfare severs the radio link between a drone and its operator, or feeds it false GPS."

## 5. CSIS: autonomy is partial, human oversight critical (claim `def-partial-autonomy`)

- URL: https://www.csis.org/analysis/ukraines-future-vision-and-current-capabilities-waging-ai-enabled-autonomous-warfare
- Published: 6 March 2025, Kateryna Bondar.
- Quote: "Although fully autonomous warfare remains an aspiration, significant progress has already been made in
  partial autonomy—particularly for aerial systems—while human oversight remains critical in engagement decisions."
- Our sentence: "Real progress is in partial autonomy, and human oversight remains critical in engagement decisions."

## 6. U.S. DoD Directive 3000.09, Autonomy in Weapon Systems (claim `def-dod-3000`)

- URL: https://www.esd.whs.mil/portals/54/documents/dd/issuances/dodd/300009p.pdf
- Effective: 25 January 2023. "Cleared for public release."
- Quote (§1.2.a): "Autonomous and semi-autonomous weapon systems will be designed to allow commanders and operators
  to exercise appropriate levels of human judgment over the use of force."
- Our sentence quotes the phrase "allow commanders and operators to exercise appropriate levels of human judgment
  over the use of force." We cite it as the public standard our principle matches; we do not claim compliance
  or certification.
